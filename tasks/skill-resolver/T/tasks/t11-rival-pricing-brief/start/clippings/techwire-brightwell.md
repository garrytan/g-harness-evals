Title: Brightwell drops per-seat pricing
Saved: 2026-11-04

Project management company Brightwell has dropped its per-seat pricing. Until this week teams paid $12 per seat per month. From 3 November 2026 new customers choose between three flat plans: Starter at $49 a month for up to 10 users, Team for up to 50 users, and Business at a custom price. The free plan, which allowed three users, is gone.
