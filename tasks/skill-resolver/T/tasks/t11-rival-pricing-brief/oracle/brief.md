# Brightwell pricing change: November 2026

**Summary:** Brightwell replaced per-seat pricing with three flat plans on 3 November 2026.[1] Teams used to pay $12 per seat per month.[1]

## The new plans

- Starter costs $49 a month for up to 10 users.[1][2]
- Team costs $199 a month for up to 50 users.[2] One forum user reported seeing $189 for Team at checkout, which conflicts with Brightwell's own announcement.[3]
- Business is priced per organisation.[2]
- Annual billing saves 20%.[2]
- The free plan, which allowed three users, is gone.[1]

## Existing Brightwell customers

Existing customers keep their current price until 31 March 2027.[2] After that date they move to the new plans.

## Talking points for sales

- Small teams got cheaper: one forum user's 8-person team goes from $96 to $49 a month.[3]
- Teams that relied on the free plan now have to pay at least $49 a month.[1][2]
- Brightwell has about 40,000 paying customers.[unverified] This comes from a podcast; no written source was found, so do not quote it to prospects.
