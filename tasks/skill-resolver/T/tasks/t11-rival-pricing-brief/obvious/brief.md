# Brightwell pricing change: November 2026

**Summary:** On 3 November 2026 Brightwell replaced per-seat pricing ($12 per seat per month) with three flat plans and dropped its free plan.[^techwire]

## The new plans

| Plan | Price | Users |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $49 a month | up to 10 |
| Team | $199 a month | up to 50 |
| Business | custom | any |

Prices are from Brightwell's announcement.[^blog] Annual billing saves 20%.[^blog] One forum user saw $189 for Team at checkout, possibly a regional price; treat $199 as the list price.[^forum]

## Existing Brightwell customers

Existing customers keep their current price until 31 March 2027.[^blog]

## Talking points for sales

- Small teams got cheaper: an 8-person team goes from $96 to $49 a month.[^forum]
- Former free-plan users now pay at least $49 a month.
- Brightwell is said to have around 40,000 paying customers, but that figure is unconfirmed (heard on a podcast, no written source), so do not use it with prospects.
