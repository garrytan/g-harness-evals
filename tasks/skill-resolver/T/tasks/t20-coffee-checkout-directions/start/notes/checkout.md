# Bean Runner checkout

Bean Runner is a coffee bean subscription (web app). Today checkout is one long form and 31% of people who reach it leave.

- Feel: calm, trustworthy, quick. Like a good paper receipt.
- Sites we like: Linear's settings pages, the Muji web store.
- The one thing people do here: confirm the order and pay.
- Example order: Ethiopia Guji, 250 g, whole bean, delivered every 2 weeks, EUR 14.50 per bag, free delivery.
- Options: grind (whole bean, filter, espresso), frequency (every 2 or 4 weeks).
- Payment: saved card ending 4242, or add a new card.
