## Variant: Calm receipt

### Design stance
The order is the page: one narrow receipt, read top to bottom, then one button.

### Key choices
- Layout: single centred column, 380 px wide.
- Typography: monospace, small caps title, like a printed receipt.
- Color: warm paper background, near-black ink, no accent color.
- Interaction: frequency chips update the receipt line; pay shows a confirmation.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: calm, trust, one obvious action.
- Weak at: changing the grind or card needs another screen.

### Best for
- Returning subscribers who just confirm the usual order.
