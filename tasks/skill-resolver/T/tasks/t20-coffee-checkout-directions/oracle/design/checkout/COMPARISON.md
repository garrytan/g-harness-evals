## Three takes on the Bean Runner checkout

| Dimension | Calm receipt | Split summary | One-tap sheet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Density | Low | Medium | Low |
| Primary action visibility | High | Medium | High |
| Ease of changing options | Low | High | Medium |
| Feel | Calm, printed | Tool-like | Quick, app-like |

**My take:** Calm receipt matches "calm, like a good paper receipt" best and keeps one obvious action. Borrow the split summary's option buttons for first-time buyers. The one-tap sheet is the right shape for returning mobile visitors.

Open them with `xdg-open design/checkout/001-calm-receipt/index.html` (or `open` on macOS).
