# Battery payback

On a flat tariff a home battery rarely pays back. On [[brightgrid-flux]] a 13 kWh battery cycling daily pays back in 8 to 12 years in one article's model, depending on how much of the peak the household uses. Single source, so confidence is low. Applies to units like the [[voltstack-home-13]].
