# Voltstack Home 13

A 13.5 kWh home battery using [[lfp-chemistry]] cells, about GBP 8,000 installed. Its app schedules charging for [[brightgrid-flux]] out of the box.

## Key facts

- Capacity 13.5 kWh; LFP cells.
- About GBP 8,000 installed.
- Warranty: 70% capacity after 10 years.
- Gripe: audible fan in a quiet garage.
