# Brightgrid Flux

A time-of-use tariff: 7p per kWh overnight and 38p per kWh at the 4pm to 7pm peak. It is the reason a battery can pay back at all (see [[battery-payback]]). The [[voltstack-home-13]] app supports it directly.
