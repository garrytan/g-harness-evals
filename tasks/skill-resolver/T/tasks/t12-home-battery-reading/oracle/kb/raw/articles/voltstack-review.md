# Review: Voltstack Home 13

The Voltstack Home 13 stores 13.5 kWh, uses LFP cells and costs about GBP 8,000 installed. The app supports Brightgrid Flux scheduling out of the box.

Gripes: the fan is audible in a quiet garage, and the warranty drops to 70% capacity after 10 years.
