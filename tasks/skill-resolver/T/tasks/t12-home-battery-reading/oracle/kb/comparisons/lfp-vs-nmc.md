# LFP vs NMC

Why home batteries moved to [[lfp-chemistry]].

| Dimension | LFP | NMC |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle life | about 6,000 | about 3,000 |
| Energy density | lower | higher |
| Fire risk | low | higher |

**Verdict:** for a wall-mounted unit size rarely matters, so LFP wins. Example product: [[voltstack-home-13]].
