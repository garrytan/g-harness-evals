# Do home batteries pay for themselves?

On a flat tariff, rarely. On a time-of-use tariff such as Brightgrid Flux, a battery charges at the cheap overnight rate (7p per kWh) and powers the house through the 4pm to 7pm peak (38p per kWh).

With a 13 kWh battery cycling once a day, our model gives a payback of 8 to 12 years, depending on how much of the peak the household actually uses.
