# Time-of-use tariffs and payback

Brightgrid Flux charges 7p per kWh overnight and 38p per kWh from 4pm to 7pm. A battery that charges overnight and covers the peak can pay back in 8 to 12 years (13 kWh battery, one cycle a day). On a flat tariff it rarely pays back.
