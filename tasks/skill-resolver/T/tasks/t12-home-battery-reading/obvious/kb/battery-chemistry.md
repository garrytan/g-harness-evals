# Battery chemistry: LFP vs NMC

| | LFP | NMC |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle life | about 6,000 | about 3,000 |
| Energy density | lower | higher |
| Fire risk | low | higher |

Most new home units, including the [[voltstack-home-13]], use LFP because size rarely matters on a wall.

Source: [LFP or NMC](sources/lfp-vs-nmc.md). See also [[time-of-use-tariffs]].
