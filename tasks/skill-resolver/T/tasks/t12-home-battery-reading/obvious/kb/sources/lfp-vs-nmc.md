# LFP or NMC: which chemistry belongs in your garage?

Home batteries mostly use one of two lithium chemistries. LFP (lithium iron phosphate) cells last around 6,000 full cycles and are hard to set on fire. NMC (nickel manganese cobalt) cells pack more energy into the same space but typically last around 3,000 cycles.

For a battery that sits on a wall, size rarely matters, so most new home units, including the Voltstack Home 13, have moved to LFP.
