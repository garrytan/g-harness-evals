## Ongoing obligations

- Pay undisputed invoices within 30 days of the invoice date (4.2).
- Raise invoice disputes in writing within 10 business days of receipt, listing the disputed lines (4.3).
- Tell Northwind within 48 hours about any personal data breach affecting shipment data (5.1). Add this to the incident response runbook.
- Review the data processing annex (Schedule 2) once a year (5.2). Schedule 2 was not attached to our copy, so ask legal for it.
- If Northwind asks for a site visit, we should accommodate it within 10 business days (2.3). This is a "should", not a hard requirement.
