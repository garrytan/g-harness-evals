# Master Services Agreement: Northwind Freight and Larkspur Goods

Effective date: 2026-11-01. Signed by both parties on 2026-10-28.

## 1. Definitions

"Customer" means Larkspur Goods Ltd. "Provider" means Northwind Freight Inc. "Business Day" means Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays.

## 2. Onboarding

2.1 Customer must deliver a forecast of monthly shipment volumes to Provider within 15 Business Days of the Effective Date.

2.2 Customer shall nominate a named operations contact in writing before the first shipment.

2.3 Provider may request a site visit to Customer's warehouse. Customer should accommodate such a request within 10 Business Days.

## 3. Insurance

3.1 Customer must maintain goods-in-transit insurance of at least GBP 250,000 per consignment and provide a certificate to Provider by 2026-12-01.

## 4. Invoicing and payment

4.1 Provider invoices monthly in arrears.

4.2 Customer must pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of the invoice date.

4.3 Customer must raise any invoice dispute in writing within 10 Business Days of receipt, citing the disputed line items.

## 5. Data protection

5.1 Customer must notify Provider of any personal data breach affecting shipment data within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.

5.2 Each party shall review the data processing annex (Schedule 2) once a year.

## 6. Term and renewal

6.1 The initial term is 24 months. The agreement renews for 12 months unless either party gives written notice at least 90 days before the end of the term.

Schedule 2 (data processing annex) is referenced in 5.2 but was not attached to this copy.
