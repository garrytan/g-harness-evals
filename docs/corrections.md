# Corrections

Moved here from the front page ([../README.md](../README.md#corrections)) on 2026-11-08. Its links now resolve from docs/, and one sentence about the old front page is now in the past tense. Nothing else changed.

**We keep every mistake visible.** Each affected report keeps its original figures, labelled, beside a dated correction, and this list collects them, newest first. The biggest one: ten results we had called confirmatory were later openings of a held-out set, so they are dev. No number changed; what changed is what the rows can support.

Where a wrong number was caught by review before it reached main, the record is the pull request, and the entry says so.

- **frontier-harness, 2026-11-10: a 15 s egress cut, Pi's lost task, and two runs we can't size.** Our Harbor egress sidecar closed any connection whose reply had not started within 15 s, and the run still paid for the calls it cut. Of the run's 20 cells only Hermes Agent on GPT-6.1 Sol was cut (39 requests in 11 trials, 1.78% of its spend); without those charges its cost per solved task moves from $0.4324 [$0.3114, $0.5954] to $0.4247 [$0.3052, $0.5854] and its cost ratio against Codex from 1.28x [1.05x, 1.54x] to 1.25x [1.04x, 1.51x]. Only 7 of the 39 were resent and 32 were not, so removing all $0.68 assumes each lost answer was replaced by work costing at least as much: these figures are the upper bound, and the true effect lies between removing $0.10 and removing $0.68. No verdict or rank changes either way. The README's footnote that Hermes Agent's Sol cost range clears Pi's was true only of the published figures; the ranges overlap under the 2026-11-09 correction and at the 15 s cut's upper bound, but not at that cut's lower bound alone ($0.4313 [$0.3108, $0.5940] against Pi's top of $0.3068), and no rank moves. Pi made 0 requests on pytorch-model-recovery in all 7 runs, so the preregistered rules leave the task out of Pi's pairs (the frontier-harness results, #337); scored as Pi fails, Pi's pass rate moves from 81.4% to 79.8% on Opus and from 80.4% to 78.8% on Sol, and both still pass within 15 points (Holm-adjusted p 0.043 on Opus). opus-gstack-follow and investigate-gate ran behind Harbor's own sidecar and kept no per-request logs, so they can't be sized. Reports and trials.jsonl are unchanged. [Correction with every old and new number](corrections/2026-11-10/README.md).
