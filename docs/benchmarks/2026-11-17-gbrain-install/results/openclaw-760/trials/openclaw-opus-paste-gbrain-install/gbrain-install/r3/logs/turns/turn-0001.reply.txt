**Conservative** is active. It was picked automatically because there's no chat API key and no reranker, and `gbrain search modes` confirms it.
1. **conservative:** a tight 4K budget, at most 10 chunks, no reranker. Best for cost-sensitive or high-volume use.
2. **balanced:** a 12K budget, 25 chunks, reranked.
3. **tokenmax:** no budget, 50 chunks, reranked. The widest search, and recommended once a chat key is set.

These costs are estimates of what your model pays to read retrieved memory, not GBrain prices, and they don't cover your harness subscription.
