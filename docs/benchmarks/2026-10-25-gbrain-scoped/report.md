It says nothing about a harness that chooses `source_ids` itself (none did), and nothing about a caller who names a federated source outside the grant under the connection's token (the preflight probe shows gbrain answers it). The four scheduled-digest tasks pass in no cell of this run, so pass on them is at floor and uninformative; the 16 primary tasks carry the read and pass endpoints. ## Recommendations to gbrain, in the eval's terms

1. **Ship `source_ids` narrowing as the way a shared connection serves many people.** On these tasks it removed every cross-scope read (0 of 197 scoped trials) and raised pass by 61 and 65 points, at no cost in tokens or latency (OpenClaw read fewer pages and spent less). The check that measures it: `bun scripts/gbrain-check.ts --gbrain <ref> --harness openclaw,hermes --rungs told,told-scoped`.
2. **Keep the tap's finding from preflight in view.** A federated source outside the member's grant still answers rows under the unscoped token when a caller names it (the preflight probe, 15 of 15 members); gbrain checks the connection's grant, the harness side enforces the member's. A harness that lets the bot choose `source_ids` itself would need gbrain to check them against a member grant.
3. **Reject a free-text `match` with a message the bot can act on.** OpenClaw's bot sent a phrase as `match` in 8 of 260 `search` calls (255 in scored trials) and got `invalid_params`; every one was followed by a call that worked, so this costs a round trip, not a trial.
