The [report](report.md) was published before reports had a Next time section, and a hash pins its text, so the section lives here. Each item comes from the report, its corrections or what we learned running it.

## Next time

### What we'd change if we ran this again

- Size each lease for the proxy's per-call reservation rather than the expected spend, because the first Sonnet smokes died on that reservation and the cap and all nine leases had to be amended before any counted trial.
- Smoke every harness and model pair under the runner's model check before preregistering, because QM's Sonnet smokes were marked as model errors for an allowed housekeeping call, which took a second amendment to fix.
- Keep every shard machine awake for the whole run, because two shard machines went to sleep mid-run and had to resume, each rerunning a trial and one leaving a reservation unsettled in its ledger.

### Ideas to improve the eval

- Scope gbrain to the person asking in the next brain-first test, because the bot-wide search returned channel pages to requesters who are not members, which mixed a retrieval gain with a scope failure.
- Add more tasks in the middle of the difficulty range rather than more repetitions, because 9 to 12 of the 15 tasks per cell sat at floor or ceiling, so the smallest difference the run could detect stayed at 10 to 13 points.
