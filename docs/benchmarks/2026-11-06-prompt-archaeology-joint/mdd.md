Generated by [build-prereg.ts](build-prereg.ts) from launch 3 of prompt-archaeology-2026-11 ([results/trials.jsonl](../2026-11-01-prompt-archaeology/results/trials.jsonl)).

- **Method:** scripts/prompt-archaeology/tables.ts familyPooledMdd, the earlier run's method: each task at its family's pooled pass rate, paired variance 2p(1-p) per trial, the task-clustered error sqrt(g/(g-1) x spread / n); power 0.8; pass-rate points. It is the arithmetic of [the earlier run's mdd.md](../2026-11-01-prompt-archaeology/mdd.md), and the build checks that it reproduces `familyPooledMdd` at that run's own design.
- **Planning input:** launch 3 of prompt-archaeology-2026-11 (results/trials.jsonl): each pair's full cell pooled with its cut cell (P1: the two single-layer cells, memory-skills and tool-descriptions, since no launch 3 cell removed both), 20 v0.3 tasks x 2 repetitions per cell. The spread per trial is the mean over tasks of 2p(1-p).
- **Alpha:** Holm over the 4 primary pairs: the smallest p is tested at 0.05 / 4, the level a lone significant pair must meet. The alpha 0.05 column is the last Holm step, for reference.
- **Design:** 4 repetitions; 20 tasks when all five families are in, 12 when the gate keeps the minimum of three.
- **Chance a zero-effect cut clears -10:** chance that the 95 percent pass interval lower bound lies above -10 points when the true delta is 0, normal approximation. That is the chance a truly harmless cut earns the confirmed CUT-CANDIDATE line.
- **Caveat:** v0.3 sat near the ceiling, so its spread is small. The gate keeps only pairs whose full arm is between 20 and 80 percent, which means more spread on v0.4. The two bound rows show what that does.
- **Recompute:** Before the main stage the spread is recomputed from the probe (each family at its chosen level, the pooled full-cell rate of that harness) with the same formula, and written to the run log beside decision.json before any main shard starts.
